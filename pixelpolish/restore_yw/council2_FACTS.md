## Описание под ролик (черновик)
Sixth-plate daguerreotype, hand-colored, c. 1855. Stamped on the brass mat: F. Grice, Rochester, New York. Her name was never recorded — the plate was never signed, and the archive catalogued it simply as [Unidentified young woman, half-length portrait, facing front]. A daguerreotype was made on a silvered copper sheet, and the exposure took tens of seconds. That is why nobody smiles on these plates: they simply could not hold one that long. 171 years of oxidation bloom, scratches and silver mirroring, removed layer by layer. Source: Library of Congress, DAG no. 1502. Public Domain. Restored with AI. The face and the sheer yoke are reconstructed, not recovered — the original plate is too faded to carry fine detail.
