# PixelPolish — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-29_

**PixelPolish Publisher** is a personal publishing tool. It posts portfolio
content (images, titles, descriptions) created by PixelPolish to PixelPolish's
own Pinterest business account via the official Pinterest API.

## What data we collect

None. The application:

- does **not** collect, store, or process any personal data of Pinterest users;
- does **not** read data from any account other than our own;
- only **writes** our own content (pins and boards) to our own account.

## Third parties

No data is shared with, sold to, or received from any third party.
